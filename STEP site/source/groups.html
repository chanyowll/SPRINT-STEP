The cohort

STEP Groups

Ten research teams from eight institutions, from Baguio to Davao, each bringing a technology of their own making into the program. Teams keep working from their own laboratories for the whole cycle — SPRINT-STEP travels to them.

What this page does not show

Each team holds a technology that is still being protected. This page carries the team name, the institution behind it and a single line describing the work — nothing on methods, results, data or filings. Team members, scores and outputs stay inside My Team's Work, which only that team can open.
