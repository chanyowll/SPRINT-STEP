What teams leave with

Three things, every cycle

Nothing here stays on a slide. Every weekly output feeds one of these three, so by Demo Day they are finished rather than started.

01

A validated business model

Not a guess about who might buy. A beachhead market you chose, sized, and then checked with the people actually in it.

  • Beachhead market and customer segments
  • TAM, SAM and SOM, with the reasoning behind each
  • Quantified value proposition, Lean Canvas, go-to-market plan
Weeks 1 to 6
02

A FASTRAC proposal

The DOST form that funds the next stage — answered a few items a week, so none of it is written the night before the deadline.

  • All 24 items of DOST FASTRAC Form 2
  • A technology roadmap you can defend
  • A two-year budget that adds up
Assembled across the whole cycle
03

A path to spin-off

Where the technology can go and what stands in its way, written down while the people who can answer are still in the room.

  • Draft IP documentation or strategy
  • Freedom-to-Operate analysis of the real risks
  • A pitch deck built one slide a week
Weeks 7 to 12

Each one is scored by a three-person panel before the cycle ends, so what you leave with has already been read by people outside your team.

          Program brief

          What STEP is for

          The Science and Technology Entrepreneurship Program (STEP) equips scientists and engineers with the entrepreneurial skills to create sustainable business models, prepare successful FASTRAC grant proposals, catalyse the establishment of spin-off companies that commercialize their technologies, and foster a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship that contributes to Philippine economic development.

          STEP is the second phase of SPRINT — the Short-term Program for Researchers on Innovation and Technopreneurship — and follows the Filipinnovation Entrepreneurship Corps (FEC). It is funded by DOST-PCIEERD and implemented by the Ateneo Intellectual Property Office.

          01

          Entrepreneurial skills

          Train researchers to apply what they learned in FEC and build sustainable business models in a short, focused time frame.

          02

          FASTRAC proposals

          Guide each team through the preparation of a DOST FASTRAC grant proposal.

          03

          Spin-off companies

          Foster the creation of spin-offs that commercialize the innovations developed by scientists and engineers.

          04

          Innovation culture

          Promote innovation and entrepreneurship in the Philippines and contribute to economic development.

          Training setups

          Five ways teams learn

          Online · weekly

          Online learning sessions

          One three-hour Tuesday morning over Zoom, one module at a time, taught by faculty from the Ateneo School of Management and invited specialists. Lecture, discussion, and a worked exercise on the team's own technology — you leave with the week's work already started.

          • WhenTuesdays, 9:00 AM–12:00 NN
          • FormatZoom, recorded — watch it later if you cannot join
          • After eachOne team output, uploaded by Friday
          Per team · weekly

          Mentoring sessions

          Every team is paired with a mentor drawn from business practitioners and faculty. One hour a week to work through the assignment, prepare for Saturday, and decide what to test next.

          • WhenAny slot the team picks, Wednesday to Friday
          • Length1 hour, online
          • MentorsOne assigned mentor for the whole cycle
          Saturdays · panels A, B and C

          Feedback sessions

          Teams play a five-minute video of the week's output to a panel of three entrepreneurs and faculty, then take fifteen minutes of questions. The comments become the team's short list for the week ahead — nothing to write up afterwards.

          • Format5-minute video, then 15 minutes of Q&A
          • Your slot20 minutes — you do not sit through the whole morning
          • Panel3 panelists per session, rotating
          On site · two days

          On-site workshops

          Mid-programme, everyone gathers at the Ateneo campus for the hands-on modules: intellectual property and patent searching with IPOPHL, financial analysis, tax incentives for spin-offs, and the team organization and spin-off simulation.

          • CoversModules 7 to 10
          • IncludesTeam building, exhibits, networking lunch
          • 2025 cycleSeptember 16–17
          On site · closing

          Demo Day and graduation

          The cycle closes with a pre-Demo Day video pitch, then Demo Day itself in front of investors, DOST officials, agencies and industry partners — followed by the FASTRAC writeshop, awarding and graduation.

          • Panel6 Demo Day panelists, 3 for the pre-Demo Day
          • AlsoTechnology roadmapping, FASTRAC writeshop
          • 2025 cycleOctober 14–15
