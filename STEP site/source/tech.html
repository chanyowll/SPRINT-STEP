Panels

Set who sits on each Saturday panel and which teams it hears. Drag a team or a panelist to another panel to move them. A mentor can't sit on the panel that hears their own team — the board won't allow it. What you save here is the grouping the Panel tab shows.
Every panel, including any you add, gets its own score sheet with the same criteria as the others. Its panelists fill it in on the Panel tab.
